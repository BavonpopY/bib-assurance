About us

Protecting families across Thailand

BIB Assurance — Bangkok Insurance & Benefits — provides health, critical illness, life, personal accident and retirement protection for individuals and families.

We believe insurance should be simple to understand, fairly priced and easy to use when it matters most. Every plan can be compared, purchased and managed online, and every claim is assessed by a dedicated claims officer.
